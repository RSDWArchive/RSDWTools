How do I use this?

Click on the plus (+) icon in the Quest Editor and select your Save file.

Check incomplete quests you want marked complete, then hit Save.


Your Save file is here (paste this into your file explorer):

%localappdata%\RSDragonwilds\Saved\SaveCharacters\


Disclaimer: v1 marks overall quest completion only. It does not grant quest rewards or reset quests.
